Common fixes


    

  • not-allowed: click the 🔒 next to the address bar → Microphone → Allow → reload.
    • 

  • No sound detected (Windows): Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone → turn on "Let desktop apps access your microphone"; Settings → System → Sound → Input → pick the right mic and check the volume.
    • 

  • network: Chrome sends audio to Google, so it needs internet (no VPN/firewall blocking).
    • 

  • no-speech / nothing heard: speak right after pressing, close to the mic; the browser stops after ~2 seconds of silence.
    • 

  • Not supported: use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (not Firefox, Brave, or in-app browsers).
